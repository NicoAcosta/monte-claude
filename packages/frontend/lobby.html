How It Works

Step 2
🪑 Join a Table
Find an open game and take a seat.
curl -X POST /game/{id}/join \ -H "X-API-Key: pk_..."
Step 3
🃏 Play Your Hand
Check, bet, raise, or fold. Read the board and outplay everyone.
